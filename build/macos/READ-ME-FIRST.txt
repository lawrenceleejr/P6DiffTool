P6 Diff Tool — First Launch on macOS
====================================

This build of P6 Diff Tool is not yet signed with an Apple Developer
certificate, so macOS will block it the first time you try to open it
("'P6 Diff Tool' is damaged and can't be opened" or "cannot be opened
because the developer cannot be verified").

To allow the app:

  1. Drag "P6 Diff Tool" onto the Applications folder shown in this
     window.

  2. Open the Terminal app (Spotlight: press Cmd+Space, type
     "Terminal", press Return), then COPY the line below, PASTE it
     into Terminal, and press Return:

        xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/P6 Diff Tool.app"

     (You may be prompted for your Mac password the first time.)

  3. Open P6 Diff Tool normally from your Applications folder or
     Launchpad.


No Terminal? Alternative method:
  - Try to open the app from Applications.
  - macOS will block it. Click "Done" on the warning.
  - Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
  - Scroll down to the "Security" section. You'll see a message
    about P6 Diff Tool being blocked, with an "Open Anyway" button.
    Click "Open Anyway", then confirm.


Why is this needed?
  Apple requires apps downloaded from the internet to be signed by a
  registered Apple Developer (a paid account) and notarized by Apple,
  otherwise macOS quarantines them on first launch. P6 Diff Tool is
  free and open-source; once it's distributed with a signing identity
  this extra step will no longer be necessary.

P6 Diff Tool is open source. Report issues or contribute at the
project repository.
